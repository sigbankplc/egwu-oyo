About Us

Quality • Reliability • Professional Service

The Blessed Egwu Oyo Enterprise is a recognized leader in palm oil meal processing and agro-industrial supply in Nigeria and across West Africa. With years of hands-on industry experience, our company has grown into one of the most trusted suppliers of Palm Kernel Cake (PKC), palm oil meal, and other essential agricultural by-products used by feed mills, manufacturers, livestock farms, and industrial processors.

Our operations are supported by modern extraction and drying systems, clean processing environments, and a dedicated workforce committed to delivering products that meet national and international quality standards. We maintain strong relationships with farmers, processors, transporters, and exporters, ensuring reliable supply and consistent product quality throughout the year.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality palm oil by-products and agro-raw materials through modern processing technologies, ensuring consistent value, superior quality, and reliable supply to our customers locally and internationally.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted and preferred provider of palm oil meal and agro-industrial materials in Africa, setting industry standards through innovation, integrity, and operational excellence.

Our Core Values

  • Integrity: We operate transparently and ethically.
  • Quality Commitment: We ensure superior product standards through continuous testing and inspection.
  • Innovation: We adopt modern technologies to enhance production efficiency.
  • Customer Focus: We prioritize customer satisfaction and long-term partnerships.
  • Reliability: Timely deliveries and consistent supply form the backbone of our operations.

Why Choose Us?

  • Premium-Grade Products: Our PKC and palm oil meal undergo strict quality control.
  • Modern Technology: We use efficient processing and drying equipment.
  • Experienced Team: Professionals with deep industry knowledge oversee production.
  • Reliable Supply Chain: We maintain stable year-round availability.
  • Competitive Pricing: High value at fair and transparent prices.
  • Nationwide Delivery: Fast and secure logistics across Nigeria and beyond.

Our Industry Impact

Over the years, The Blessed Egwu Oyo Enterprise has contributed significantly to the livestock and agricultural industries by ensuring steady supply of nutritional palm by-products used in feed formulation. Our products support poultry farmers, feed manufacturers, fishery farms, cattle ranchers, and industrial processors who rely on consistency and quality to run their operations effectively.
