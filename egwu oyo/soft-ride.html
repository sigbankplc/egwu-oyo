Market Opportunity

Nigeria’s transportation industry serves over 200 million people and remains one of the most essential sectors for mobility. With increasing road travel, limited airline affordability, and expanding urban-to-urban migration, demand for safe, modern, and reliable interstate transportation continues to rise.

Soft Ride is strategically positioned to capture this growing demand by offering a superior travel experience supported by technology, comfort, and exceptional customer service.

Target Customers

Our services are designed for:
  • Business professionals traveling between major economic hubs.
  • Students commuting to institutions across different states.
  • Families and individuals seeking safe and comfortable travel.
  • Corporate organizations requiring staff shuttle or charter services.
  • Logistics clients needing interstate parcel movement.
These segments make up over 70% of the interstate mobility market in Nigeria.

Business Model & Revenue Sources

Soft Ride operates a hybrid travel and logistics business model designed for sustainability and scalability.

Revenue is generated through:
  • Interstate passenger transportation tickets
  • Corporate travel and staff shuttle contracts
  • Bus charter services for groups, events, and organizations
  • Logistics and parcel transport services
  • Value-added services (onboard WiFi, priority boarding, premium seating)

Pricing Strategy

Our pricing is competitive while maintaining high-quality service standards. Prices are determined by:
  • Distance and route demand
  • Vehicle class (Standard, Premium, Executive)
  • Seasonal changes and peak travel periods
We blend affordability with comfort, making Soft Ride accessible to both premium and budget-conscious travelers.
